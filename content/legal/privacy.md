# Privacy Policy — Floowtix AI Messenger

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

Floowtix ("we", "us") provides an AI assistant that answers customer messages on Facebook Pages of businesses that hire us ("our clients"). This policy explains what information we handle when you message one of those Pages, and how.

## 1. Information we receive
When you send a message to a Facebook Page that uses our service, Meta Platforms sends us:
- your Page-scoped user ID (a number Meta assigns for that Page)
- the content of your messages, including text and the type of any attachment
- the time of each message

If you choose to place an order, you may give us your **name, mobile number, delivery address, district**, the product variant or color you want, and your payment method (for example, cash on delivery).

We do not receive your Facebook password, your friends list, or any information you did not send to the Page.

## 2. How we use it
- to reply to your questions about the business's products, prices, delivery and policies
- to record your order and pass it to the business so they can confirm and deliver it
- to hand your conversation to a human staff member when needed
- to send you one reminder inside Messenger's 24-hour messaging window if you asked about a product and did not finish
- to produce aggregated statistics for the business (for example, number of messages and orders)

We do **not** sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who processes it
- **The business you messaged** — sees your messages, order details and contact information so they can serve you.
- **Google (Google Sheets)** — stores the business's order and conversation records.
- **Our automation server (n8n)** — processes messages.
- **AI language model providers via OpenRouter** — receive the text of your message to generate a reply. They are not permitted to use it to identify you.
- **Meta Platforms** — delivers messages between you and the Page.

## 4. How long we keep it
Conversation and order records are kept while the business uses our service, and for at most 12 months after that, unless the business must keep order records longer by law. Technical logs are kept for at most 90 days.

## 5. Your choices and rights
- You can ask for a human at any time by writing "human" or "মানুষ".
- You can ask us to **delete your data** — see our [Data Deletion Instructions](https://floowtix.com/data-deletion).
- You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing us.

## 6. Security
Access to stored records is limited to the business and Floowtix staff who operate the service. Credentials are stored encrypted, and all connections use HTTPS.

## 7. Children
The service is not intended for children under 13.
